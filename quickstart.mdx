TruHuddle gives your organization a publication managed from one workspace. Start here to set up your blog and publish your first article.

1. Create your account and verify the email code. Select or create the workspace that owns your blog.
2. Open **Billing**. Eligible new accounts can start one three-day Startup trial without a card. Plans and credits belong to the selected workspace.
3. Open **Blog Settings**, choose the appearance and free address, and save each settings tab.
4. Create an article, preview it, then publish when it is ready. Drafts stay private.

Startup includes publishing, custom domains, team members, advanced analytics, Markdown copying and usage-billed AI features. Enterprise adds API access, custom templates and branding removal. See [pricing](https://truhuddle.com/pricing) for the full comparison. Buying AI credits does not activate a paid plan.

Owners and administrators manage billing and developer credentials. Writing and settings access follow the role assigned within the current workspace. Switching workspace also switches its plan, content and credit balance.
