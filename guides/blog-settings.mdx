---
title: "Customize your blog settings"
description: "Configure your publication appearance, navigation, theme, favicon and social previews, then enable reader features and save changes to your blog."
keywords: ["blog settings","theme","favicon"]
---

**Appearance:** choose a primary color, heading/body fonts and Light, Dark or System mode. Configure the header title, description and navigation links.

**Features:** choose article sharing, Markdown copying, AI destinations, author information, updated dates, external links opening in a new tab, and whether cards without thumbnails keep or hide their image area. Public AI and voice reading require explicit fee confirmation.

**Configurations:** set the blog SEO title, description, social image, logo text/link, light/dark logos and favicon. Add footer links and connect a custom domain. A feature can also require an active workspace plan.

Save Changes on the tab you edited. Updates invalidate the affected resource caches; saving a logo does not purge every cached article. An already-open browser page may need a refresh to display its new server-rendered settings.
