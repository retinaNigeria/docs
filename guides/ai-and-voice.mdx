---
title: "Use AI writing, reader chat and voice"
description: "Draft and revise articles with AI, enable chat over your published content, and let readers listen to your posts using workspace-funded voice playback."
keywords: ["AI writing","reader chat","voice reading"]
---

In the editor, select text and open the AI tool to request a revision, or ask for a full draft. Review the generated text before publishing. The dashboard assistant can create drafts and answer bounded workspace questions; it cannot publish on your behalf.

To enable visitor chat, turn on **AI Conversations** in Blog Settings, accept the usage notice and save. Readers see a separate AI button beside search. The assistant searches only this workspace’s published articles and returns links to supporting posts.

For **Listen to Page**, select the allowed languages and a male or female voice, confirm the fees and save. A reader chooses a language and plays the article in streamed sections, with pause and stop controls. The current paragraph is highlighted; word highlighting is an estimate where the browser supports it.

Public chat, audio and translation use the publication workspace’s credits. Usage limits protect the balance. When access or credit is unavailable, visitors receive a neutral unavailable message. Cached audio replays do not make a new generation request; editing the article selects fresh audio.
