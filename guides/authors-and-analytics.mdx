---
title: "Manage author profiles and analytics"
description: "Show public author profiles with job titles and bios, then view workspace and article traffic reports with advanced analytics on Startup or Enterprise."
keywords: ["authors","job title","analytics"]
---

Set your name, profile photo, job title and About me in **Profile**. With Show Author enabled, clicking the byline opens a profile containing those public fields and that author’s published articles. Empty job titles and bios are hidden. Account email and platform access grants are never part of the public profile.

Author URLs use names, for example `/authors/samuel-ntekob`. Same-name authors receive a numeric suffix. Recent previous profile addresses redirect after a name change.

Advanced audience and article analytics are available on Startup and Enterprise. Open Dashboard for the publication report, or an article’s analytics page for that post. Choose 7, 30 or 90 days. Reports load on demand; refresh the app for newer data. Privacy preferences and blockers can prevent tracking, so analytics totals can differ from billed page deliveries.
