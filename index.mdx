## Before you begin

Your selected workspace owns its blog, plan, members and AI credit balance. Startup includes publishing, a custom address and advanced analytics. Enterprise adds API access, branding removal and custom templates. Eligible new accounts can start a three-day Startup trial without a card.

Start with the [quickstart](/quickstart), or check [plans and credits](/guides/plans-and-credits) before enabling usage-billed features.
